1. Company understanding

Zenith is the parent AI/digital innovation brand: AI & ML solutions, digital products, and culture-led marketing. Its ecosystem includes fashion intelligence (J.E.R.K.), education (AI4Education), music (MusicLabel AI), AI literacy (AI Insight), and e-commerce (Baby Dino).

J.E.R.K. is the consumer-facing fashion + culture AI product: trend forecasting, live signals, AI look recommendations, colour/palette direction, and industry intelligence.

Together they sit at the intersection of content, code, and culture — strong positioning for Gen Z fashion-tech.

2. What is already working

Zenith

  • Clear AI + culture positioning
  • Portfolio narrative connecting multiple products under one engineering brand
  • Services story (chatbots, CV, NLP, custom ML) matches intern role expectations

J.E.R.K.

  • Strong brand voice (“read the room, read the future”)
  • Concrete AI outputs: look match scores, season/occasion, silhouette, palette, “why this works”
  • Product surface already supports Colour Analysis / fashion AI storytelling
  • Free plan mentioned — good for acquisition if made more visible

B. J.E.R.K. product & Colour Analysis AI

  1. Make Colour Analysis the hero loop — selfie → undertone/season → 6–8 hex palette → 2–3 outfit recommendations (best Instagram demo).
  2. Show method, not only claims — Add source categories, refresh time, and confidence on Trendwall cards.
  3. India-context styling — Office / festive / wedding / campus occasions and Indian wardrobe constraints.
  4. Feedback loop — Track save / wear / reject on looks → improve ranking; publish match-quality over time.
